Privacy

This page covers the portenv.com website and the usage numbers the Portenv app can send.

The website

  • The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking.
  • It loads nothing from other sites: no fonts, scripts or images from third parties.
  • It is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To deliver and protect the site, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, as described in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Usage numbers from the app

The Portenv app is counted in usage numbers only if you tick “Count me in usage numbers”. It's off until you tick it, on the first screen and in Settings › Updates, and one click there turns it off again. While it's off, the app sends nothing.

While it's on, the app sends one check to portenv.com at most once a day, only on days you open a workspace, with these fields and nothing else:

  • Portenv's version, the macOS version (only the major one, like 26) and the Mac's architecture;
  • the month you installed Portenv;
  • whether it's the first check this week, and the first this month;
  • once a week: whether a workspace moved, whether an agent worked in a workspace, and rough counts of your workspaces (1, 2–5 or 6+) and connected servers (0, 1, 2–3 or 4+).

Never sent: any ID, your name or email, workspace names, file names or anything in your workspaces. The check sets no cookie.

Your IP address is seen in transit but never kept. The check is received by a Cloudflare Worker that reads only these fields and adds one to a daily count for each field's value; its logs are off. Only those daily totals are kept, never a record of a single check. Cloudflare acts as our processor under its data processing agreement, with its EU-US Data Privacy Framework certification as the basis for transfers.

Any figure we show outside the company hides counts below 10.
