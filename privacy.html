Privacy

This page covers the portenv.com website and the Portenv app's update check.

  • The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking.
  • It loads nothing from other sites: no fonts, scripts or images from third parties.
  • It is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To deliver and protect the site, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, as described in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The app's update check

The Portenv app checks for updates at most once a day, and only on days you open a box. The check sends no identifier of any kind: no install ID, no account, nothing about your work, your boxes' names or their files.

With “Count me in usage numbers” on, the check also carries these fields, and nothing else:

  • the app's version, your macOS version and your Mac's architecture;
  • the month Portenv was installed (for example October 2026);
  • whether this is the first check of the week, and of the month;
  • once a week: whether a box was moved that week, whether an agent worked in a box that week, how many boxes you have (1, 2–5 or 6 or more) and how many servers are connected (0, 1, 2–3 or 4 or more).

The check goes to portenv.com, which sends the app on to GitHub for the update feed and the download. portenv.com keeps only totals for each field, never a record of each check, and no IP addresses. Cloudflare, which hosts portenv.com, and GitHub see your IP address in transit, as described in Cloudflare's privacy policy and GitHub's privacy statement.

To stop counting, untick “Count me in usage numbers” on the Welcome screen, or later in Portenv's Settings. The update check itself stays on, so you still get security updates.
