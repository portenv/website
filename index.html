Questions

What is Portenv?

A shared workspace for AI work. Your project lives in one encrypted workspace you own; AI agents work in it together, each with its own role and access, and you approve what matters. It runs on your Mac or your own server.

Who is it for?

Right now, independent developers and small teams who already use AI agents and want them to do more without handing over their machine.

Which agents and models does it use?

Claude Code agents work in it today; ChatGPT joins through MCP, Grok through the terminal, more as they open up. You set each agent's model in its agent file.

Can the agents touch my Mac or my files?

Not outside the workspace. Inside it, each agent has its own copy of your code: it can read your project but not change it, and it doesn't get your sign-ins, SSH keys or git credentials, so it can't push. It can still read files in your project that aren't in git, such as a .env file, so keep secrets out of the project for now. Its work comes back to you as a branch you review.

Can I undo what an agent did?

Yes. Every workspace keeps encrypted save points. Go back to any earlier one, from your Mac or your server.

Do I need my own AI subscription?

Yes. Each agent signs in to Claude Code once, in its own terminal, with your own Claude or Anthropic Console account. Portenv doesn't resell model access. Sharing one sign-in between agents isn't in the tester build yet.

Does my code leave my computer?

Your workspace runs on your Mac, or on your own server if you move it there. Saves are encrypted on your Mac first. The agents send what they work on to Anthropic, as Claude Code does anywhere.

What does it cost?

Portenv is free during testing. Your agents' model use is billed to your own Claude or Anthropic account, as usual.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 26 or later, Docker Desktop installed and open (Portenv runs workspaces in it for now), and a Claude account for your agents. A GitHub account is optional: sign in from the app to clone and push your own repositories.

Does Portenv collect data about me?

No. The tester build collects no usage numbers. If Portenv ever counts usage, it will ask you first, and it will never include anything about your work.

Is it open source?

Yes. The Mac app, the runner, the in-workspace agent and the workspace format are all in one public repository under Apache-2.0.
